About

Several fields, one practical focus.

I work primarily as a Senior Lecturer in Radiography at UCL University College Denmark. I teach AI, imaging optimisation, radiation protection and MRI diagnostics, and I contribute to technology coordination, health-data research and the practical introduction of AI in education and healthcare.

I have university degrees in Software Construction and Clinical Biomechanics. I also hold Danish clinical authorisation with permission for independent practice and was positively assessed as a Danish lektor in 2020.

I am currently completing an MSc in AI, Technology and Sustainability, which includes Le Wagon's Data Science and AI Bootcamp.

“I am interested in technology that can improve quality of life, but only when safety, privacy, evidence and wider consequences are considered from the start.”

SIRT: Safety in Imaging and Radiation Treatment

I contribute as a researcher to this project on occupational ionising-radiation exposure and long-term health outcomes. Karen Brage is the principal researcher (PI) and Pernille Lund Hansen is CoPI. A central part of the work is the shared development of researchable register data based on occupational exposure records held by the Danish Health Authority’s Radiation Protection unit, alongside collaboration with Danish and international research partners.
