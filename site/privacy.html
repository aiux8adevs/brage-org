Who is responsible. This site is run privately by Oliver Brage, Odense, Denmark. Contact: contact (at) brage.org.

No cookies or tracking. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising, and stores nothing on your device. Fonts are served from this site, not from a font service. In the simulators, your choice of language and level is kept in the page address, not saved in your browser.

Server logs. The site is hosted by Simply.com in Denmark. Like any web server, it records technical information about each request, such as IP address, time, the page requested and browser type. These logs are used only to run and secure the site. Simply.com processes them on my behalf as a data processor. The legal basis is legitimate interest in operating a secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Links. Links to other sites, such as ORCID, are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

Your rights. You can ask for access to or deletion of data about you, or object to its processing, by writing to the address above. The supervisory authority is the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).
